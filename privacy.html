Privacy Policy

Calm Maze · Last updated 6 October 2026

In short: Calm Maze doesn't collect any personal information, from children or from grown-ups. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your child's progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Calm Maze is a gentle puzzle game for young children, made by Conway Tong. If you have any questions about this policy, email us at ctpentagon@hotmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Calm Maze doesn't ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it doesn't send information about you or your child to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To remember where your child is up to, the app saves a small amount of information only on the device it's played on:

  • which mazes are finished, and the explorer, hats, glasses and stickers chosen
  • acorns and the items placed in the den
  • grown-up settings, such as sounds and vibrations

This never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The first 10 mazes are free. Unlocking the full game is a single, one-time purchase (there is no subscription), handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details. The app is only told whether the full game has been unlocked, so it knows which mazes to open. On a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app's grown-ups area.

No ads, analytics or third parties

Calm Maze has no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party services built in. It doesn't use your information to build a profile or follow your activity across other apps or websites.

Printables

Activity packs and certificates are created on your device. They're only printed or shared if you choose to, using your device's own Print or Share options.

Children's privacy

Calm Maze is made for children aged 6 to 8. Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't collect it from children. Purchases, settings and any links that leave the app are behind a parent passcode, so children can't reach them by accident.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Calm Maze handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app. We'll never start collecting personal information from children.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Email ctpentagon@hotmail.com.
