Help & Support

Calm Maze, a gentle puzzle game for children aged about 4 to 7.

Need a hand? Email ctpentagon@hotmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

How do I unlock all 100 mazes?

The first 10 mazes are free. To open the rest, choose a subscription on the "Unlock all 100 mazes" screen. A grown-up will be asked a quick question before buying.

I've already subscribed. How do I get the mazes back?

Open Calm Maze, tap the padlock at the top of the home screen, answer the grown-ups question, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel or change a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions and choose Calm Maze. Your child's progress is kept either way.

Can I turn the sounds or vibrations off?

Yes. Tap the padlock on the home screen, answer the grown-ups question, and use the switches under Settings. Sounds also follow the device's silent switch.

How do I start again from Level 1?

In the same grown-ups area, tap Start Over. This clears finished mazes, outfits, acorns and the den, but not your subscription.

What information does Calm Maze collect?

None. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
