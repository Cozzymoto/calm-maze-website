Help & Support

Calm Maze, a gentle puzzle game for children aged 6 to 8. Younger children can play the early mazes with a grown-up.

Need a hand? Email ctpentagon@hotmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

How do I unlock all 100 mazes?

The first 10 mazes are free. To open the rest, tap "Unlock all mazes" on the "Unlock all 100 mazes" screen. It's a single payment, not a subscription, and it's yours to keep. A grown-up will be asked for the parent passcode (or a quick question, the first time) before buying.

I've already unlocked the mazes. How do I get them on a new device?

Open Calm Maze, tap the padlock at the top of the home screen, then tap Restore Purchases and enter the parent passcode. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. With Family Sharing, the mazes also unlock on your family's devices.

I've forgotten the parent passcode. What do I do?

On the passcode screen, tap Forgot passcode?. Answer the grown-ups question (a multiplication written out in words), then choose a new 4-digit passcode. Your child's progress and your purchase aren't affected.

How do I change the parent passcode?

Tap the padlock at the top of the home screen, enter the parent passcode, then tap Change Parent Passcode.

Is there a subscription?

No. Unlocking the full game is one payment. There's nothing to cancel, and your child's progress is kept either way.

Can I turn the sounds or vibrations off?

Yes. Tap the settings gear on the home screen, enter the parent passcode, and use the switches under Settings. Sounds also follow the device's silent switch.

How do I start again from Level 1?

In the same grown-ups area, tap Start Over. This clears finished mazes, outfits, acorns and the den, but not your purchase.

How can I rate Calm Maze?

Thank you! Tap the padlock on the home screen, enter the parent passcode, then tap Rate Calm Maze. Ratings help other families find the game.

What information does Calm Maze collect?

None. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
